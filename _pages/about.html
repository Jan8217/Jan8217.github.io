About me

I’m an AI researcher at the Artificial Intelligence Institute of the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT). I received my M.Sc. in Medical Engineering and Assistance Systems from the Technical University of Munich, where I focused on AI and machine learning.

My recent research includes long-video understanding at Guangming Laboratory with Prof. Wenhao Jiang and multi-agent systems for dentistry at Tsinghua University’s College of AI with Prof. Miao Liu.

Research interests

  • Long-video understanding
  • Multimodal reasoning and agents
  • Evidence-grounded AI for public health
